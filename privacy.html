CometChat Inc., doing business as Crossword ("Crossword", "we", "us" or "our"), provides Crossword, an artificial intelligence platform that helps businesses engage, support and sell to their customers. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and share Personal Information through the Crossword websites, dashboard, widgets, agents, APIs and any other sites or services that link to this Privacy Policy (together, the "Services"), and the choices available to you.

Crossword is the controller of Personal Information that we collect for our own purposes, for example from visitors to our websites, prospective customers and users of the Crossword dashboard. When a business uses the Services, for example by deploying a Crossword agent or widget on its online store or connecting a platform such as Shopify, we process Personal Information about that business's customers and visitors ("End Users") on that business's behalf, and that business's own privacy policy applies to it, except where this Privacy Policy describes our own use of that information (see Section 3). If you are an End User, please direct questions and requests to the business you interacted with.

1. Personal Information We Collect

"Personal Information" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with or linked to you or your household. Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

(a) Contact and account information, such as your name, email address, phone number, company, job title and login details, including where you sign in through a third-party service;

(b) Financial information, such as billing details collected through our payment providers;

(c) Usage and device information, such as IP address, device and browser type, referring URLs, approximate location and information about your interactions with the Services;

(d) Communications, such as the content of your emails, chats, calls and video calls with us (which we may record), and any files you share;

(e) Customer and End User information, such as conversations between End Users and Crossword agents, End User activity on sites where Crossword is installed, and information made available through platforms our customers connect, such as product catalogs and inventory, orders and order history, purchase and checkout events, shipping addresses, and customer names and contact details;

(f) Employment information, if you apply for a role with us; and

(g) Inferences drawn from any of the above.

2. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect Personal Information directly from you; automatically, through cookies and similar technologies (see Section 5); from our customers and the platforms and systems they connect to the Services; and from third parties such as business partners, analytics and advertising providers, data enrichment and website visitor identification providers, and publicly available sources.

3. How We Use Personal Information

To the extent permitted by applicable law, we may use Personal Information to: provide, operate, maintain and secure the Services; create and manage accounts and fulfil requests and purchases; provide customer support; communicate with you, including about our products, services and promotions; personalize the Services and our marketing; analyze usage, benchmark and conduct research; protect against fraud, abuse, security incidents, claims and other liabilities; meet our contractual obligations; comply with law and enforce our terms and policies; evaluate job applicants; and for any other purpose disclosed at the time of collection or otherwise permitted by law.

Developing and training our AI. We may de-identify Personal Information, including conversation content and other information processed through the Services on behalf of our customers, and use the de-identified information to develop, improve and train our products, services and artificial intelligence models, as permitted by our agreements with our customers and applicable law. We remove information that identifies individuals before using it to train our models, and our customers can opt out of this use.

We may also create de-identified or aggregated information from Personal Information and use it for any lawful purpose. We maintain and use de-identified information in de-identified form and do not attempt to re-identify it, except as permitted by law.

4. How We Share Personal Information

We do not sell Personal Information. We may share Personal Information with: (a) service providers that help us operate our business and the Services, such as hosting, AI model, payment, communications and analytics providers; (b) the customer whose Services you use, if you are an End User; (c) our affiliates; (d) advertising, analytics and visitor identification partners, with your consent where required by law; (e) other parties where we believe it is reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, enforce our terms, or protect the rights, property or safety of Crossword, our customers or others; (f) another party in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, spin-off or sale of all or part of our business; and (g) other parties with your consent or at your direction.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We and our service providers use cookies, pixels, local storage and similar technologies to operate the Services, keep you signed in, remember your preferences, understand how the Services are used and, where permitted, support our marketing. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before placing non-essential cookies, and you can change your choices through the cookie settings on our websites or your browser settings. We honor Global Privacy Control signals where required by law.

When a customer installs Crossword on its site, our widget may use cookies or local storage on that site to operate the Services and remember conversations, and may collect End User activity on that site. The customer is responsible for obtaining any consent required from its End Users.

6. How Long We Keep Personal Information

We keep Personal Information for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or longer where required or permitted by law. In deciding how long to keep it, we consider its nature and sensitivity, the purposes for which we hold it, our legal obligations, and the potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure. Personal Information we process on behalf of a customer is kept in accordance with our agreement with that customer. De-identified or aggregated information, and AI models trained using de-identified information, may be kept after the underlying Personal Information is deleted.

7. How We Protect Personal Information

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect Personal Information, including encryption and access controls. Crossword is covered by CometChat's information security program, which is independently audited against recognized industry standards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of Personal Information.

8. International Transfers

We are based in the United States, and we, our affiliates and our service providers process Personal Information in the United States, India and other countries, which may have data protection laws different from those where you live. Where required, we protect transfers with appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your Personal Information; to restrict or object to certain processing; to withdraw consent; to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sharing" of your Personal Information; to opt out of certain automated decision-making; and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. You may opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in them; we may still send you service-related messages.

To make a request, email us at the address in Section 15. We will verify your identity before fulfilling most requests, and an authorized agent must provide proof of authority. We may deny or partly fulfil a request where permitted or required by law. Requests to opt out of sharing do not require verification. If you disagree with our response, you may appeal by emailing us with the subject line "Privacy Request Appeal", and then contact your local regulator. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

If you are an End User, please make requests to the business you interacted with. We will assist that business as required by our agreement with it and applicable law.
